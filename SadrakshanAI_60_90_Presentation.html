03 — HOW IT WORKS

The Automated Alert Escalation Chain

4 / 9
📋 Step 1 — Data Entry
FIR details entered by PS staff: FIR No., BNS Sections, IO Name, FIR Date, Crime Category, E-Saksha, FSL status, and chargesheet details.
↓
⚙️ Step 2 — Automated Engine
The backend automation runs daily. It calculates investigation days remaining for each FIR and identifies which milestone threshold each case has reached.
↓
📲 Step 3 — Instant Alerts
At defined milestones, instant mobile alerts are sent to the IO's Police Station, SDPO, and Addl SP — escalating progressively based on urgency.
↓
📡 Step 4 — Dashboard Review
The live HTML dashboard fetches the latest data automatically. SDPO reviews performance across all 15 PS and tracks all alert cases in real time.
