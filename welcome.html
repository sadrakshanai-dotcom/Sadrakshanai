About This Platform

Why SadrakshanAI Exists

Maharashtra Police handles over a lakh FIRs every year across 1,100+ police stations. Most of what works — and what doesn't — stays within individual stations and never travels further.

SadrakshanAI was built to change that. It is a documentation platform where good policing practices, investigation lessons, and public awareness content are written down, published, and made available to every officer and citizen in the state.

No corporate backing. No advertising. Built by officers, for officers and the public.

Knowledge Documentation

Capturing investigation practices and learnings that would otherwise stay locked in individual stations.

Public Accountability

Transparent documentation of good work builds citizen trust in policing institutions.

Performance Visibility

Real-time dashboards and data-driven reporting across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.
