BACKGROUND

The Problem This System Solves

🚫

No real-time visibility

Officers at division level had no way to know which FIR investigation was running behind — unless they physically checked registers at each station.

📋

Manual follow-up was unreliable

Reminders to IOs were verbal or on paper. There was no guarantee an IO was alerted at Day 14 or Day 28 of investigation.

🔒

No accountability trail

When a task was marked done, there was no record of who updated it, when, and at what stage — making reviews difficult.

📊

Senior officers had no live picture

The Addl. SP and SDPO had to rely on verbal reports during meetings. No live status of all 15 PS investigations was available.

✅  Sadrakshan.AI was built to solve all four of these gaps — automatically, reliably, without adding extra work for any field officer.

STEP 2 & 3

Auto-Detection and Alert to SDPO

Every morning — automatic scan
The system scans the master register and identifies every FIR where investigation has reached exactly Day 14 or Day 28.
Alert prepared automatically
For each such FIR, the system prepares a detailed alert message with FIR number, BNS section, IO name, police station and days count.
SDPO's mobile receives the alert
The alert is delivered to the SDPO's mobile — instantly, even if they are in the field. No human has to send this message.
Pre-filled review form attached
The alert includes a button. SDPO taps once — the review form opens with FIR details already filled in. SDPO only marks tasks.
✅  Both Khamgaon SDPO and Malkapur SDPO receive their respective cases separately — each only sees cases from their sub-division.

STEP 6

Day 28 Review — with Full Day 14 History

28

The most powerful feature

When Day 28 arrives, the SDPO does not need to remember what was done on Day 14. The system carries it forward automatically.

Day 14 answers are pre-loaded
When SDPO opens the Day 28 form, all 22 tasks from Day 14 are already visible — what was completed and what was still pending.
SDPO only updates what changed
Tasks completed between Day 14 and Day 28 can be updated. Tasks that were done on Day 14 remain locked — no duplicate entry needed.
PSO again notified of remaining pending tasks
If any tasks are still pending at Day 28, the PSO gets a second, stronger alert — clearly showing what the IO failed to complete since Day 14.
🔴 If tasks are still pending at Day 28, it means the IO ignored the Day 14 instruction — this creates a clear accountability record visible to the SDPO.

SUMMARY

Complete Picture — Who Receives What & When

📱 SDPO
WHEN: Day 14 & Day 28 of each FIR
  • FIR details and BNS section
  • Pre-filled review form — tap once to open
  • On Day 28: Day 14 answers are already loaded
  • Separate alerts for Khamgaon and Malkapur cases
  • Alert delivered even when in the field
📱 PSO / Station House Officer
WHEN: Immediately after SDPO submits review
  • Exact list of all pending tasks (numbered)
  • Count of completed tasks
  • Clear instruction: report back to SDPO
  • If all done: congratulatory confirmation message
  • Each PS gets its own alert — no confusion with other PS
📱 Addl. Superintendent of Police
WHEN: Every evening at 8:00 PM
  • Total active FIRs across all 15 PS
  • Cases at Day 14 and Day 28 today — by name
  • Division-wide pending task count
  • Chargesheet filed count
  • No report writing — fully automatic
📋 IO (Investigating Officer)
WHEN: Via PSO, immediately after review
  • Exact list of tasks that must be completed
  • No ambiguity — each task is clearly named
  • Clear accountability: must report back to SDPO
  • Prior completed tasks remain permanently locked
  • Second stronger alert if tasks still pending at Day 28
🔒

Every completed task is time-stamped and permanently locked — no one can alter or delete it. This creates a clean, tamper-proof investigation record from Day 1 to chargesheet filing.
